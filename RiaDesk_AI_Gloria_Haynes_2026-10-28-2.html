Starter ideas for your brands

01
“Loc Myth vs. Fact”Short videos answering one question at a time: washing locs, lint, buildup, moisture and retightening. Check claims against reliable sources before posting.
02
“What I noticed during a loc consultation”Explain common maintenance issues without exposing a client's identity or making a diagnosis.
03
“Ingredient spotlight”Introduce one ingredient, its traditional use, what evidence exists, and what is still uncertain. Avoid presenting hair-growth or medical claims as proven without adequate evidence.
04
“Your loc-care routine, simplified”A practical weekly or monthly care checklist, adjusted for loc maturity, scalp needs and professional advice where needed.
05
“Ask a loc specialist”Invite followers to submit questions, then turn the most useful answers into short educational posts and approved FAQs.
Trend Lab starter ideas are suggestions, not verified claims about what is trending today. Use the research shortcuts to check current conversations and sources before building a campaign.
